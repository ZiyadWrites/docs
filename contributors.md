# Contributors

## Core Contributors

### Turbofish

Nomic’s founders and core contributors are Turbofish. Turbofish are building the next generation of open-source blockchain technologies, focusing on security, decentralization, and efficiency.

Turbofish was founded in 2018 by ex-Cosmos/Tendermint employees [Matt Bell](https://twitter.com/mappum) and [Judd Keppel](https://twitter.com/juddkeppel).

You can learn more about Turbofish at [turbofish.org](https://turbofish.org/)
